DATA DICTIONARY
Raw values can intentionally fail these target types.

CUSTOMERS
One customer profile per customer_id before exact replays. Required keys must be usable; city and email can have repairable quality issues.
  customer_id | Text | Customer business key, such as C000001.
  customer_name | Text | Synthetic display name; may contain CSV quotes or commas.
  email | Text, optional | Synthetic email; invalid values must be flagged and nullified.
  city, state | Text | Customer location. City can be blank, lower-case or padded.
  signup_ts | UTC timestamp | Customer creation time in 2024; initial history starts here.
  segment | Text enum | NEW, REGULAR or VIP.
  is_active | Boolean | CSV true/false; a soft delete later changes current state.
  source_updated_at | UTC timestamp | Source update time, not your ingestion timestamp.

CATEGORIES
One category per category_id. Four root categories and eight child categories.
  category_id | Text | Category business key.
  category_name | Text | Readable category name.
  parent_category_id | Text, optional | Another category_id; empty for a root category.

SUPPLIERS
One supplier per supplier_id.
  supplier_id | Text | Supplier business key.
  supplier_name | Text | Synthetic supplier label.
  city | Text | Supplier city.
  lead_time_days | Integer | Planning estimate, not an observed shipment duration.

PRODUCTS
One catalog product per product_id. Historical order prices come from order_items, not this catalog.
  product_id | Text | Product business key; P00001 is intentionally popular.
  product_name | Text | Synthetic product label.
  category_id, supplier_id | Text | References to categories and suppliers.
  brand | Text | Synthetic brand label.
  catalog_price_paise | 64-bit integer | Current catalog price in paise.
  unit_cost_paise | 64-bit integer | Static synthetic reference cost; insufficient for real historical margin accounting.
  is_active | Boolean | Current catalog activity. Inactive products can have valid historical orders.

STORES
One store or warehouse per store_id.
  store_id | Text | Store business key.
  store_name | Text | Synthetic location name.
  city, state | Text | Location geography.
  store_type | Text enum | STORE or WAREHOUSE.

ORDERS
One order header per order_id before exact replays. Initial and incremental records are inserts; customer history changes are in a separate CDC feed.
  order_id | Text | Order business key, such as O0000001.
  customer_id, store_id | Text | Required references to customer and store; deliberate customer defects exist.
  order_ts | UTC timestamp | Order business time; deliberately invalid text occurs in some records.
  order_status | Text enum | PENDING, CANCELLED, PAID, SHIPPED or DELIVERED.
  sales_channel | Text enum | WEB, APP or STORE.
  shipping_fee_paise | 64-bit integer | Order-level shipping fee; do not repeat it per item.
  order_total_paise | 64-bit integer | Sum of line net plus shipping, before returns. No tax component.
  source_updated_at | UTC timestamp | Time of the represented state. Keep separate from file arrival.

ORDER_ITEMS
One purchased line per order_item_id before exact replays. An order can have several lines for the same product.
  order_item_id | Text | Line business key, such as I0000001_1.
  order_id | Text | Parent order key.
  product_id | Text | Product key; P99999 is an intentional missing product.
  quantity | Integer | Purchased units; must be positive after validation.
  unit_price_paise | 64-bit integer | Price per unit at purchase time.
  discount_paise | 64-bit integer | Total line discount, bounded by quantity times unit price.

PAYMENTS
One payment attempt or refund per payment_id. An order can have a failed attempt, a successful charge, and a later refund.
  payment_id | Text | Payment-event business key; do not deduplicate by order_id.
  order_id | Text | Parent order key.
  payment_ts | UTC timestamp | Payment event time; use this for cash-date reports.
  payment_type | Text enum | CHARGE or REFUND.
  payment_status | Text enum | SUCCESS or FAILED; only SUCCESS affects cash.
  payment_method | Text enum | CARD, UPI or NETBANKING.
  amount_paise | 64-bit integer | Positive magnitude. Payment type determines cash direction.
  related_return_id | Text, optional | Return business key for REFUND; empty for CHARGE.

SHIPMENTS
One shipment per shipment_id in this dataset, at most one per order. Production designs may need split shipments.
  shipment_id | Text | Shipment business key.
  order_id | Text | Parent order key.
  carrier | Text | CarrierA, CarrierB or CarrierC.
  shipped_ts | UTC timestamp | Shipment departure time.
  promised_delivery_ts | UTC timestamp | Original promised deadline.
  delivered_ts | UTC timestamp, optional | Actual delivery time; blank while in transit.
  shipment_status | Text enum | IN_TRANSIT or DELIVERED.

RETURNS
One return per return_id, referring to a single item. Current data has at most one partial return per selected order, but your tests must cover cumulative limits.
  return_id | Text | Return business key.
  order_item_id | Text | Returned item; derive order_id through the item relationship.
  return_ts | UTC timestamp | Return event time, after delivery.
  return_quantity | Integer | Positive returned units.
  refund_amount_paise | 64-bit integer | Approved merchandise refund; shipping is excluded.
  reason | Text enum | DAMAGED, WRONG_SIZE or CHANGED_MIND.
  return_status | Text enum | APPROVED in this source version. Do not assume future statuses are automatically approved.

INVENTORY
One snapshot per snapshot_date, store_id and product_id. Coverage: June 1-30, stores S001-S004, products P00001-P00500.
  snapshot_date | Date | Calendar date of the stock snapshot.
  store_id, product_id | Text | Composite key components and dimension references.
  on_hand_quantity | Integer | Available units, including valid zeros.
  reorder_level | Integer | Threshold used for low-stock reporting.

WEB_EVENTS
One event per event_id before exact replays; malformed physical lines have no usable event key. JSON Lines format.
  event_id | Text | Event business key. Preserve source record identity for corrupt lines.
  session_id | Text | Source session key; some abandoned sessions have no customer.
  customer_id | Text or null | Optional logged-in customer ID.
  event_type | Text enum | view_product, add_to_cart, checkout or purchase.
  event_ts, received_ts | UTC timestamp | When the activity happened and when this export received it.
  order_id | Text or null | Present for purchase events only; non-purchase events are not order lines.
  product_id | Text | Viewed/primary product; not a full purchased-cart measure.
  device | Object | type: mobile/desktop/tablet; os: Android/Linux/iOS.
  marketing | Object | source: organic/email/paid_search; campaign: text or null.
  cart | Array of objects | Each element has product_id (text) and quantity (integer); can be empty.
  schema_version | Integer | 1 in baseline/early batches; 2 from July 4 batches.
  app_version | Optional text | Added in version 2; absent in version 1, not a parsing failure.

CUSTOMER_CDC
One source change event per change_id before exact replays. These are synthetic file-based change events, not captured database logs.
  change_id | Text | Unique change identity; use to recognize replay.
  customer_id | Text | Customer whose state changes.
  operation | Text enum | I = insert, U = update, D = soft delete.
  effective_ts | UTC timestamp | When the new business state becomes valid.
  source_sequence | 64-bit integer | Ordering/tie-breaker from the source. Equal timestamp and sequence with different payload is a conflict.
  received_ts | UTC timestamp | When the change arrived. Arrival order can differ from effective order.
  before | Customer object or null | Context only; not guaranteed to be the immediately prior image.
  after | Customer object or null | Full authoritative image for I/U, null for D. Contains customer fields from the customer dictionary.
